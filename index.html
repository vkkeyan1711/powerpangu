Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you get started.

Are the scanners and courses really free?

Yes. The scanner links are free to open. To get the free training courses, open your Zerodha account through the PowerPangu link and message on WhatsApp.

How do I open a Zerodha account?

Click any Open Zerodha Account button. You will be taken to Zerodha's official page to complete the online process.

Do I need trading experience?

No. The courses start from the fundamentals and build step by step.

Can I get support after opening my account?

Yes. Lifetime support is available on WhatsApp.

Are the scanners buy or sell recommendations?

No. Scanners only filter stocks by technical conditions. Always do your own analysis and manage risk.
